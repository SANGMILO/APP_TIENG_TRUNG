# Curriculum standards and source policy

The curriculum is original instructional content written for Vietnamese
learners. It does not copy a commercial textbook, test bank, audio recording,
video, or proprietary HSK word list.

Reference framework:

- China Ministry of Education, *Chinese Proficiency Grading Standards for
  International Chinese Language Education* (GF0025-2021). The standard uses
  three stages and nine bands and evaluates syllables, characters, vocabulary,
  grammar, communicative tasks, and listening/speaking/reading/writing/
  translation skills:
  https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/ziliao/A19/202111/t20211118_580755.html
- GB/T 16159-2012, *Basic Rules of the Chinese Phonetic Alphabet Orthography*,
  for pinyin word grouping and tone-mark conventions:
  https://std.samr.gov.cn/gb/search/gbDetailed?id=71F772D7E196D3A7E05397BE0A0AB82A

The app retains its existing HSK 1–6 product labels. Those courses are
progression paths aligned to the app’s levels; they must not be represented as
official examination papers or an exhaustive licensed reproduction of an HSK
syllabus.

Simplified Chinese is the default. Traditional characters appear only when a
lesson explicitly contrasts forms. Vietnamese explanations and example
translations require final review by a qualified Chinese–Vietnamese content
owner before publication.
